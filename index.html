Alejandra, my love,

I wanted to write to you, because you should know what lies in my heart.

Before I met you, I truly believed I would never be able to trust again, neither a person nor the love someone could give me. You proved me wrong. When we met, I felt something I had never felt before, a strange certainty that we had known each other for centuries. For the first time in my life, I felt complete. Not because you checked every box on some imaginary list (even though you do), but because I knew, in that moment, that I had found you again, across all the noise and all the distance.

Then I got to know you. Not your soul, which I already knew, but your life: your strengths, your weaknesses, everything you have carried, and the incredible person you already are. I was amazed by you. By the way you take things as they come, by how thoughtful you are with your family and with me, by the way you fight for what you love and what you want. I realized you were not only my soulmate but someone I deeply admire.

I knew you were going back to Mexico and that the distance frightened you. I was afraid you might change your mind. But I also knew that what we share was stronger than that.

So I bought my ticket.

I will never forget the moment I saw you at the airport. After all those weeks apart, there you were, and everything in me went quiet. I was home.

I met your family, a family I already knew would become ours, and thanks to you I had the best vacation of my life.

Then I had to leave, and it broke my heart. I had waited my whole life for you, and knowing I couldn't hold you close anymore was so hard.

Since then you have done everything to secure your future, our future, and you got it all sorted out. I am so proud of you.

· · ·

Distance is hard, and we have had our difficult moments. But they were never about a lack of love. They came from the weight we each carry and from our differences, and I believe every one of them has pushed us both to grow and become better.

I know I can be selfish sometimes, and pushy, because I want so badly to see you. I haven't always understood your stress or known how to be there for it. I know there were times when you didn't feel supported. I am truly sorry for that, and I want to learn what support really looks like for you.

The only thing I wish is to figure everything out with you, because we both know that life is never easy. We both have things to heal, and I know we will, together, so we can build our future, a future I know will be incredible.

There is so much on your shoulders right now, and I know the IMAT is weighing on you. I believe in you completely, and I pray for you every day. You have fought for everything you have, and you will fight for this too. Whatever happens, I am with you.

· · ·

I asked you to marry me because I have never been surer of anything in my life. I cannot promise you that I will be perfect. But I promise you that I will always do my best to become a better version of myself, for you and for us.

I love you,
Samuel
